# K-pop Crossword

Canonical: https://kpopquiz.online/en/crossword/
Also in Portuguese: https://kpopquiz.online/pt-br/cruzadinha/

Use the clues to write the group names in the grid. Each clue gives
one or two facts about the group, such as its formation year, record label or
a member, and the names cross at shared letters. Leave out spaces, punctuation
and accents, but keep numbers. Check your answers as often as you like, or
reveal them.

A new grid comes out every day, the same for every player, with no group from
the day before. On a day without a verified grid, there is no crossword.
Progress is saved in the browser.

## Provenance and license

Every clue points at a Wikipedia revision (CC BY-SA 4.0,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), with the page, the revision
number and the position of the quoted passage. The selection rejects a clue
that fits more than one group in the collected catalog. Group identifiers come
from Wikidata (CC0, https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Copyright).
