# K-pop Timeline

Canonical: https://kpopquiz.online/en/timeline/
Also in Portuguese: https://kpopquiz.online/pt-br/linha-do-tempo/

Order the K-pop events from earliest to latest.

Daily challenge with events from K-pop history. Arrange them in
chronological order and reveal exact dates backed by verifiable sources.
